Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit these pages, the host processes technically necessary access data that your browser transmits automatically:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • requested file or URL
  • amount of data transferred and status code
  • browser type, browser version and operating system
  • where applicable, the previously visited page (referrer)

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in the technically error-free operation and security of the website. This data is not combined with other data sources and not used to build usage profiles. The retention period is determined by the host.

Where the host processes personal data on my behalf, a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place. Any transfer to a third country is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

Encryption

The pages are served over HTTPS (TLS), so the connection between your browser and the server is encrypted.

Contact by email or phone

If you contact me by email or phone, your details, including your contact information, are stored to handle your enquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering the enquiry) or, if the enquiry concerns a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for that purpose and no statutory retention periods apply.

External links

This site links to external services (including GitHub, LinkedIn, press pages and my own projects). Their operators alone are responsible for data processing on those sites. They are only contacted when you actively click a link; no content from these providers is loaded automatically.

Your rights

You have the right at any time to

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise these rights, use the contact details above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; in Bavaria this is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 27, 91522 Ansbach.
