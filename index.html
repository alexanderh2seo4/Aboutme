JAR Medical / TriARge — AR-assisted triage

Explores a digital alternative to paper triage tags: ArUco markers on patients, an AR overlay for responders, and a local incident-command hub with speech recognition and radio logging. Runs fully offline on the incident-site network; cloud sync is optional. Prototype for training and simulation, not a certified medical device.

jar-medical.tech / Python, FastAPI, OpenCV, faster-whisper, SQLite

JAR Medical website · Incident-command demo

ems-voice — voice documentation for EMS

A pipeline from speech to a structured patient care report: transcription, phonetic correction of German drug names and medical terms, normalisation of numbers and units, extraction into a fixed schema. Built for a 6 GB RAM budget on a headset — with its own training data and an evaluation that measures not word error rate but whether the medical terms survive.

jar-medical.tech / Whisper, LoRA, Kölner Phonetik, GBNF grammar

Speech recognition demo

InnovAlloy DeepDive

Analysis of phase-associated brittle fracture in Bi-Sn-Zn-Al systems: why an alloy that protects steel from corrosion during galvanising unexpectedly turns brittle and breaks at higher temperatures. With Levi Jekic and Lennart Antritter at Gymnasium Neubiberg.
