FPV-Power.de — drones & components

Fabian and I founded FPV-Power.de in 2022, bringing our own experience building and repairing FPV drones into a shop for components and spare parts.

From flying to supplying parts

The project grew from a familiar problem in our own hobby: a missing or broken component could mean waiting weeks before flying again. We built the shop around availability and shipping from Germany.

Running it brought hardware knowledge together with e-commerce and logistics. Choosing components and understanding repair needs mattered alongside making the ordering and fulfilment process work.

Hands-on work with drones

My broader FPV work includes repairing and custom-building drones, club activities with FPV-München, and aerial imaging. These projects connect electronics, mechanical decisions and the practical constraints of flying.

That background also connects to my software projects: Storm Patrol explores drone inspection planning, while the JAR Medical prototype explores how aerial observations can contribute to an incident map.
