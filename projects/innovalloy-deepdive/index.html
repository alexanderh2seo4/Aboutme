InnovAlloy DeepDive — Jugend forscht

With Levi Jekic and Lennart Antritter at Gymnasium Neubiberg, I investigated phase-associated brittle fracture in Bi-Sn-Zn-Al alloy systems. Our project won the 2026 Jugend forscht national Chemistry competition.

Why a protective alloy becomes brittle

The research asks why an alloy used in the context of protecting steel from corrosion during galvanising can become brittle at elevated temperatures. It connects alloy composition and phase behaviour with the way a material fractures.

The full project title is InnovAlloy DeepDive: Analyse phasenassoziierter Sprödbrüche in Bi-Sn-Zn-Al-Systemen. The work sits at the intersection of chemistry and materials research.

National Chemistry award in 2026

Levi Jekic, Lennart Antritter and Alexander Kluge received first prize in Chemistry at the 61st Jugend forscht national competition, held from 28 to 31 May 2026 in Herzogenaurach.

The Bavarian education ministry's public announcement names the three researchers, Gymnasium Neubiberg and the project. It provides an independent reference for the award and the research title.
