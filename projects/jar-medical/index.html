JAR Medical — TriARge, ems-voice & MEDICRAFT

I co-founded JAR Medical to build tools for emergency responders. The work connects offline incident coordination, augmented reality and German medical speech recognition.

TriARge: offline incident coordination

TriARge is an offline-first mass-casualty incident triage prototype. Marker-based patient identification connects an AR responder view to a local incident-command hub. The hub brings patient information, voice documentation, radio logs and a shared incident view together on the local network.

The system uses Python, FastAPI, OpenCV and SQLite, with optional cloud replication. It is a prototype for training and simulation, not a certified medical device or a system ready for real emergency operations.

ems-voice: from speech to structured documentation

ems-voice explores how spoken German emergency-care notes can become structured documentation. The pipeline combines transcription, correction of medical terminology, normalization of numbers and units, and extraction into a defined schema.

The research focuses on preserving medically relevant terms within the memory constraints of a headset. Training data and evaluation matter alongside the model itself: a transcript can look readable while still getting a drug name or a number wrong.

MEDICRAFT: speech-data collection through a game

MEDICRAFT is a browser game built around recording German emergency-domain speech. It provides a way to contribute spoken examples to the project's speech-data work.

The public contribution pages offer a game and a direct recording flow. Their own notices explain participation and recording before a visitor contributes.

Drone imagery and the incident map

Another part of the work connects drone imagery with incident maps. The aim is to place observations in a shared geographic view so that an operator can review the image evidence and its location together.

This is part of the broader incident-coordination prototype. The public demos introduce the interface; they do not establish operational readiness.
