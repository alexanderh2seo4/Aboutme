ReqFlow — BMW product requirements

Our EHL hackathon team built ReqFlow for the BMW challenge AI for Product Decision Making: a workbench connecting customer evidence with prioritized vehicle requirements.

Keeping the evidence next to the decision

ReqFlow follows the path from customer evidence to requirements that a product manager can review and approve. The prototype brings research and requirement work into one desktop application, making the relationship between a proposed requirement and its supporting material easier to inspect.

It was built by the EHL Team Eichel hackathon team for the BMW challenge. It is a team prototype, not an official BMW product.

  • ReqFlow source and project overview

Desktop prototype and implementation

The application uses Electron and TypeScript and targets Windows, macOS and Linux. Its public repository documents the product, architecture, research implementation and review workflow.

The judging guide gives a concrete route through the prototype and its requirement coverage. The product document explains what the team set out to build, which is useful context before exploring the implementation.
