Storm Patrol — WATT THE HACK

Storm Patrol turns storm reports and uncertain grid information into a risk map, then plans how a small fleet of drones could inspect power-line sections.

From a storm report to an inspection plan

The WATT THE HACK project starts with storm reports, known dead lines and a vegetation layer. It ranks areas for inspection, selects starting locations for five simulated mobile sensor packages, and plans flights around battery and charging constraints.

Each simulated observation updates the risk estimate. The operator view shows drone activity and recommendations such as sending a repair crew, seeking confirmation or inspecting a section from the ground.

A simulation with visible assumptions

The frontend uses React, TypeScript and MapLibre, with a Python FastAPI backend. An operator view follows the inspection plan; a separate setup view controls the synthetic scenario used to evaluate it.

This is a hackathon prototype. The network and faults are fictional, and the planner does not model airspace, terrain, collisions or wind. Its recommendations demonstrate the planning approach rather than a deployment-ready inspection service.
