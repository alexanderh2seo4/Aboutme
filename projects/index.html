Current work & past projects

Software, drones & chemistry projects

I’m Alexander Kluge, based in Neubiberg near Munich. My work ranges from emergency software and speech AI to drone systems, volunteer tools and materials research. This directory connects the projects with their public websites, demos, code and coverage.

Explore the projects

JAR Medical — TriARge, ems-voice & MEDICRAFT

I co-founded JAR Medical to build tools for emergency responders. The work connects offline incident coordination, augmented reality and German medical speech recognition.

Emergency software · AR · Speech AI

Storm Patrol — WATT THE HACK

Storm Patrol turns storm reports and uncertain grid information into a risk map, then plans how a small fleet of drones could inspect power-line sections.

Hackathon · Drones · Grid inspection

ReqFlow — BMW product requirements

Our EHL hackathon team built ReqFlow for the BMW challenge AI for Product Decision Making: a workbench connecting customer evidence with prioritized vehicle requirements.

Hackathon · AI · Product decisions

AFS volunteer maps & AFSER MCP

Alongside volunteering as an AFS sending coordinator, I build tools that help volunteers find relevant exchange-program tasks and understand their geographic context.

Volunteering · Maps · MCP

Super Slides — HTML presentations

Super Slides turns plain HTML sections into a web presentation and shows how the deck was read, with no runtime dependencies or hosted service required.

Open source · Presentations · JavaScript

InnovAlloy DeepDive — Jugend forscht

With Levi Jekic and Lennart Antritter at Gymnasium Neubiberg, I investigated phase-associated brittle fracture in Bi-Sn-Zn-Al alloy systems. Our project won the 2026 Jugend forscht national Chemistry competition.

Chemistry · Materials research · Jugend forscht 2026

FPV-Power.de — drones & components

Fabian and I founded FPV-Power.de in 2022, bringing our own experience building and repairing FPV drones into a shop for components and spare parts.

Co-founder since 2022 · FPV · E-commerce
